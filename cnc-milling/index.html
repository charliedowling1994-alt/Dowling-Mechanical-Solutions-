From your drawing to a machined component

If you need a subcontract milling supplier, send us the component drawing, quantity, material and required date. We review the features, workholding and inspection requirements before confirming a price and lead time.

Our milling work covers components such as brackets, housings, fixture plates and parts with pockets, drilled holes, bores and threads. A dimensioned drawing tells us which features matter to your assembly; a STEP model helps us assess the geometry and plan the machining.

3-axis and indexed 4th-axis machining

We offer 3-axis CNC milling and indexed 4th-axis capability. Indexing allows a component to be presented at different angles for features on multiple faces. We assess whether this approach suits the part when reviewing your enquiry.

  • Facing, profiling and pocket milling
  • Drilling, tapping, thread milling and boring
  • Multi-face features using indexed machining where appropriate
  • One-off parts, small batches and repeat orders

Materials and drawing requirements

We machine aluminium alloys, mild steel, stainless steel, brass, copper and engineering plastics. Include the exact material grade with your enquiry so we can account for machinability, stock availability and any material documentation you need.

Please identify critical dimensions, fits, surface finishes and any inspection reports required. Achievable tolerances and the inspection approach are reviewed for the individual component before the job is agreed.
